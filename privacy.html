Privacy Policy

THREE KINGDOMS CARDS

Effective: October 28, 2026 Last updated: October 28, 2026 Applies to: iOS, Android, Web

中文版本 ↓

The short version: Three Kingdoms Cards can be played completely offline without an account. We do not run advertising, we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell data. If you choose to sign in for cloud saves and leaderboards, we store your email address and your game progress — nothing else, and you can delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Three Kingdoms Cards is an independent game developed and published by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

Contact for any privacy question or request: ahshg686@163.com

2. Playing without an account

No account, email address, phone number or social login is required to download or play the game. Campaign progress, unlocked cards, settings and local match history are stored only on your own device. We cannot see this data and it is removed when you delete the app.

3. Optional cloud features

The game offers optional cloud save and a leaderboard. These are the only features that send data off your device, and they activate only if you choose to sign in. You can play the entire game without ever signing in.

If you sign in, we store:

DataWhy
Email address (verified by a one-time code)To identify your account and let you recover it on a new device
Game progress (campaign stages cleared, cards collected, coins, deck setup)To sync your save across your devices
Match results (win/loss counts, score)To display the leaderboard
Display name you chooseTo label your entry on the leaderboard

We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising identifier (IDFA), or any health or biometric data. We do not build user profiles and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Data we never collect

  • No advertising SDKs, no ad identifier, and no cross-app tracking. The game does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track you.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or location.
  • No payment card details — payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google (see below).

5. Purchases

The game offers a single one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the full version. All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) on iOS or Google (Google Play) on Android. We never receive your name, card number or billing address. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase succeeded, which we store locally on your device to unlock the content.

6. Where data is stored and for how long

Cloud data (only if you sign in) is stored on servers used solely to operate this game, protected in transit by HTTPS. It is retained while your account exists. Local data lives on your device until you delete the app or clear its data.

7. Your choices and rights

You can at any time:

  • Play without cloud: simply never sign in, or sign out from the in-game settings.
  • Delete your cloud account and data: email ahshg686@163.com from the address you registered with, or use the delete option in the game. We remove your account, cloud save, match history and leaderboard entry within 30 days.
  • Ask what we hold: the same address, and we will send you a copy.
  • Correct your data: you can edit your display name inside the game at any time.

If you are in the EEA, UK, California or another jurisdiction with data-protection laws, you also have the right to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local authority. Our lawful basis for cloud processing is your consent, given when you choose to sign in.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no account is needed to play, a child can use the game without providing any data. If you believe a child has created a cloud account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The only third parties involved are the app stores that distribute the game and process payments (Apple, Google) and the hosting provider that runs the optional cloud service. Their own privacy policies govern the data they handle.

10. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Access to cloud data is limited to the developer. No system is perfectly secure, but because we hold almost no personal information, the impact of any incident is limited to game progress.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else:
ahshg686@163.com

隐私政策（中文）

《三国卡兹 / Three Kingdoms Cards》（以下简称“本游戏”）由个人开发者独立开发与运营。 本政策说明本游戏会处理哪些信息、以及为什么处理。

一句话总结：本游戏无需注册即可离线游玩；不接广告、不接统计埋点、不追踪用户、不出售任何数据。 如果你主动登录云存档和排行榜，我们仅保存你的邮箱与游戏进度，且可随时删除。

一、无需账号即可游玩

下载和游玩本游戏不需要账号、手机号或第三方登录。关卡进度、已解锁卡牌、设置与本地对战记录 仅保存在你自己的设备上，我们无法看到，卸载游戏即随之删除。

二、可选云功能（仅在你主动登录后启用）

登录后我们会保存：

  • 邮箱地址（通过验证码校验）：用于识别账号、在新设备上恢复存档。
  • 游戏进度（通关关卡、已收集卡牌、铜钱、卡组配置）：用于多设备同步。
  • 对战结果（胜负场次、积分）与你设置的昵称：用于排行榜展示。

我们不收集真实姓名、住址、电话、通讯录、照片、精确位置、广告标识符（IDFA）， 以及任何健康或生物特征信息；不建立用户画像，不跨应用或跨网站追踪你。

三、内购

本游戏提供一次性买断的“解锁完整版”。支付全部由 Apple（App Store） 或 Google（Google Play） 处理，我们不会收到你的姓名、卡号或账单地址， 仅收到“购买成功”的确认，并在本机记录以解锁内容。

四、你的权利

  • 不使用云功能：不登录，或在游戏设置中退出登录。
  • 删除账号与数据：用注册邮箱发送邮件至 ahshg686@163.com，或使用游戏内的删除功能； 我们会在 30 天内删除账号、云存档、对战记录与排行榜条目。
  • 查询与更正：同一邮箱可索取数据副本；昵称可在游戏内随时修改。

五、儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童个人信息。由于游玩无需账号，儿童可在不提供任何 信息的情况下使用本游戏。若发现儿童创建了云账号，请联系我们删除。

六、第三方与安全

我们不出售、不出租、不共享你的个人信息。相关第三方仅限分发本游戏并处理支付的应用商店 （Apple、Google），以及承载可选云服务的托管服务商。所有网络通信均使用 HTTPS 加密。

七、政策更新与联系方式

政策更新时会修改上方的“最后更新”日期，重要变更会在版本说明中提示。 任何疑问或请求请联系：ahshg686@163.com
