三国卡兹 · 隐私政策

Privacy Policy · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日（本次修订：适配纯广告 IAA 版本 / V3.46）

本政策说明《三国卡兹》（又名 Iron Front，以下简称「本游戏」）如何处理你的信息。一句话版本：不注册、不登录也能玩，进度只存在你自己的设备上；游戏免费、以可选的激励视频广告支持运营；广告 SDK 会收集设备标识符与粗略位置用于广告投放与归因，我们不出售你的数据，也不会把这些数据与你的游戏进度关联。

一、我们不收集个人信息

  • 本游戏没有账号体系、没有注册、没有登录，不要求你提供姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证号、生日或任何可识别你身份的信息。
  • 我们不运营自有用户数据库，也不会把你的游戏进度上传到我们的服务器。
  • 唯一例外是下文第三、四节所述的广告 SDK 数据与可选的联机对战临时数据，两者都不包含账号或身份信息。

二、你的设备本地存了什么

游戏进度与设置保存在浏览器的 localStorage / 应用本地存储中，仅在你的设备上读写，清除应用数据或卸载即删除。主要条目如下：

存储项内容
游戏档案 sanguo.profile.v1铜钱数量、胜负场次、战役通关记录与关卡星级、章节奖励领取状态、图鉴收藏数量、三档卡包当前内容、归顺武将表与当前援军选择。
广告每日限次记录 ads按日期记录「每日免费包」激励视频是否已领取（例如 free_pack = 当天日期），用于实现「一天一次」，仅存日期字符串，不含广告内容、不含设备标识。
自定义卡组、音量与音效开关、教程标记、提示开关纯本机偏好设置。
联机重连信息仅在用过联机对战时，临时保存房间码与座位号，便于刷新后回到原对局。
离线缓存（PWA / Service Worker）首次加载后缓存游戏资源，用于断网也能玩，不包含个人信息。
存档迁移提示：游戏内「存档码」功能可以把进度导出为一串文本，粘到新设备即可恢复。该过程不经过我们的服务器，完全由你自行保管，请勿把存档码公开分享。

三、广告 SDK 会收集什么（重要）

本游戏采用纯广告（IAA）变现：全部关卡与卡牌免费开放，无内购、无充值、无付费墙。目前只有两个可选的激励视频位：结算页「铜钱翻倍」与图鉴页「每日免费包」。

  • 为展示广告、控制频次、防止刷量以及统计广告效果（归因），第三方广告 SDK 会在广告展示时收集设备标识符与粗略位置，并可能收集设备型号、操作系统版本、网络类型、语言、IP 地址推断的城市级位置、广告展示 / 点击 / 是否完整观看等交互数据。
  • 设备标识符在不同平台上可能是 OAID、Android ID、GAID、IDFA 或平台提供的匿名广告标识；粗略位置指由 IP / 网络信息推断的城市或地区级位置，不是 GPS 精确定位。本游戏不请求定位权限。
  • 这些数据由广告平台按其自身隐私政策处理，用于广告投放、频次控制与归因；我们不在自有服务器保存这些数据，不将其与你的游戏进度、卡组或存档码合并，也不用于用户画像或出售给数据经纪商。
  • 在 iOS 上，如广告 SDK 使用 IDFA 进行跨应用追踪，系统会通过 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）向你请求授权；未授权时不影响你继续游玩，仅可能影响广告的相关性。你也可以在系统设置中限制广告跟踪或重置 / 删除广告标识符。
  • 没有接入广告源的版本（例如纯静态网页版）不会显示任何广告按钮，此时不会有广告 SDK 数据收集。

五、可选的联机对战

若你所使用的版本提供本地联机对战，服务器仅为同步对局临时保存房间码、座位号、双方阵营与出牌动作，对局结束或房间关闭后即清除，不做长期留存、不用于分析、不关联任何身份信息。不使用联机功能时不会产生这部分数据。

六、儿童与未成年人隐私

  • 本游戏面向全年龄，Q 版三国卡牌对战，无血腥画面、无聊天、无用户生成内容、无充值、无抽奖付费。
  • 我们不会有意收集儿童的可识别个人信息，也不需要实名或年龄信息即可游玩。
  • 但请注意：游戏含广告，第三节所述的广告 SDK 仍可能收集设备标识符与粗略位置。监护人可通过设备系统设置限制广告跟踪、重置广告标识符，或在无广告源的静态版本中游玩（该版本不显示广告按钮）。
  • 游戏为单机玩法，无连续登录逼迫、无限时排行榜、无体力消耗、无诱导长时间在线的设计；建议监护人陪同未成年人合理安排游戏时间。
  • 若监护人认为儿童的数据被误收集，可通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会在 30 天内处理并协助向广告平台提出删除 / 重置请求。

七、数据保存期限与删除方式

  • 本地进度与设置：由你掌控。在应用 / 浏览器设置中清除本站数据，或卸载应用，即彻底删除；游戏内也提供清除本地存档的入口。
  • 广告每日限次记录：仅保存当天日期字符串，跨日自动失效，不长期留存。
  • 联机临时数据：对局结束即清除。
  • 广告 SDK 侧的数据：由第三方平台按其政策保存，我们无法直接删除；你可通过系统设置重置广告标识符、撤回追踪授权，或按该平台隐私政策行权。

八、你的权利与选择

  • 不看广告也能完整通关：两个激励视频位都是可选项，奖励仅为额外铜钱 / 一盒卡包，不影响任何关卡、卡牌或功能的可获得性。
  • 随时停止本地存储：清除浏览器 / 应用数据即可，代价是本地进度一并清除，建议先用「存档码」导出备份。
  • 控制广告个性化：在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」、Android「Google → 广告 → 删除广告 ID」等系统入口调整；未授权追踪不影响游戏内容。
  • 查询、更正、删除与投诉：由于我们不持有账号与用户数据库，不存在「查询 / 删除账号」这一操作；涉及本政策的任何请求，请通过下方邮箱联系，我们会在 30 天内答复并协助处理。

九、政策变更与联系方式

政策更新后会在本页更新「最后更新」日期；若收集范围扩大或新增第三方 SDK，会在游戏内显著位置提示，并同步更新各应用商店的隐私标签与第三方 SDK 清单。

Privacy Policy — English Summary

Last updated: 28 Oct 2026 · Applies to Iron Front (Sanguo Kazi), V3.46 · The Chinese text above is the authoritative full version; this section summarises every clause for English-speaking users and App Store reviewers.

1. No personal information is collected. There are no accounts, no registration and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, ID number or date of birth, and we operate no user database. Your game progress is never uploaded to our servers.

2. What is stored on your device. Game progress and preferences live in local storage (browser localStorage / app sandbox) only: coins, wins/losses, campaign clears and stage stars, chapter reward flags, card collection counts, the three pre-generated pack contents, recruited generals and the selected reserve general, custom decks, sound settings, tutorial flags, and an optional reconnect record (room code + seat) for local multiplayer. An ad daily-limit record stores only a date string (e.g. "free_pack = today's date") to enforce one free pack per day — it contains no ad content and no device identifiers. PWA/Service Worker caches static assets for offline play. Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes all of it. The in-game "save code" export is a text string you keep yourself; it never passes through our servers.

3. Advertising SDKs — what they collect (important). The game is free, ad-supported (IAA only), with no in-app purchases, no payments and no paywall. There are exactly two optional rewarded-video placements: "Double Coins" on the results screen and "Daily Free Pack" in the collection screen. To deliver ads, cap frequency, prevent fraud and measure attribution, third-party ad SDKs collect device identifiers and coarse location, and may collect device model, OS version, network type, language, city/region-level location inferred from IP, and ad impression / click / completion events. Device identifiers may be OAID, Android ID, GAID, IDFA or a platform advertising ID; coarse location means city/region inferred from IP or network, not GPS precise location — the game never requests location permission. These data are processed by the ad platform under its own privacy policy for ad delivery, frequency capping and attribution; we do not store them on our own servers, do not merge them with your game progress, decks or save codes, do not build user profiles, and do not sell them. On iOS, if the SDK uses IDFA for cross-app tracking, the system will ask for permission through App Tracking Transparency (ATT); declining does not affect gameplay. Builds with no ad source (e.g. the pure static web build) show no ad buttons at all and therefore collect no ad data.

4. Third-party SDK list (placeholder, to be completed per channel). Planned or possible ad networks: WeChat Mini Game Ads (WeChat), Pangle / 巨量引擎 (Douyin mini games, Android), and Google AdMob (App Store overseas, Google Play). Each channel build must mark which one is actually integrated and link its privacy policy before submission; unintegrated entries must stay marked "No". No other third-party analytics, crash, profiling or social-login SDK is included. In-game voices and sounds are bundled/ synthesised locally and never record or upload your voice.

5. Optional local multiplayer. Where available, the server temporarily holds only a room code, seat number, faction and play actions to synchronise a match; these are cleared when the match ends or the room closes, are never retained long-term, never analysed, and are never linked to an identity. Not using multiplayer produces none of this data.

6. Children's privacy. The game is suitable for all ages: chibi-style card battles, no gore, no chat, no user-generated content, no payments, no paid loot boxes. We do not knowingly collect children's personally identifiable information and require no real-name or age check. Please note the game contains ads, so the SDK data described in clause 3 may still apply; guardians can limit ad tracking or reset the advertising ID in device settings, or use the static no-ad build. The game is single-player-first with no forced daily-login streak, no timed leaderboard, no energy system and no design that encourages excessive play. If a guardian believes a child's data was collected in error, contact us and we will respond within 30 days and assist with deletion/reset requests to the ad platform.

7. Retention and deletion. Local progress and settings are deleted by clearing app/browser data or uninstalling (export a save code first as backup). The ad daily-limit record keeps only a date string and expires automatically the next day. Multiplayer data is cleared at match end. Ad-SDK-side data is retained by the third party under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID, withdraw tracking authorisation, or exercise your rights directly with that platform.

8. Your rights and choices. Watching ads is never required to finish any level or obtain any card — rewards are only bonus coins or one card pack. You can stop local storage at any time by clearing app data. You can control ad personalisation via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Android Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID; declining tracking does not change game content. Because we hold no accounts and no user database, there is no "account query/deletion" operation; for any request under this policy, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Changes and contact. We update the "Last updated" date on this page; if the collection scope expands or a new SDK is added, we will notify players prominently in-game and update the store privacy labels and SDK list. Privacy enquiries, deletion requests and minor-related requests: ahshg686@163.com (personal developer mailbox).
