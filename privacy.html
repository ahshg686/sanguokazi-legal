三国卡兹 · 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日 · 适用版本 v3.66 及以后

《三国卡兹》（英文名 Three Kingdoms Cards，以下简称“本游戏”）是一款单机卡牌对战游戏。本游戏没有账号系统，不要求注册、手机号、邮箱或密码，也不接入广告。本政策说明我们会处理哪些信息、为什么处理、保存在哪里，以及你可以怎样删除它们。

一、我们不收集的信息

本游戏不要求注册或登录，不采集姓名、手机号、邮箱、通讯录、照片、精确位置、麦克风或摄像头内容，不读取剪贴板，不申请广告标识符（IDFA），也不做跨 App 或跨网站的行为追踪。游戏音频只在本机播放，不会录音，也不会上传你的声音。

二、游戏进度保存在哪里

本机。游戏进度、卡组、已解锁关卡与星级、铜钱与外观、设置项，以及奖励领取日期，保存在设备的本地存储中，主要键名为 sanguo.profile.v1 与 sanguo.decks.v1。游戏随时可以离线玩，离线期间产生的进度只在本机。

云端备份（联网时自动进行）。为避免重装或换机丢档，本游戏会在联网时把你最近一次的游戏进度（关卡与星级、已拥有的武将、卡组、皮肤等）上传到我们使用的数据库服务，作为备份。这份备份用一串随机生成的匿名设备标识来区分，不包含你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、Apple 账户、支付卡号或广告标识符，也不会与你在其他 App 或网站上的行为关联。每台设备在同一时间只保留最新一份备份，旧的会被覆盖。

换机迁移。你可以在游戏内生成一串恢复码，在新设备上输入即可把进度、已购铜钱与月卡迁移过去。恢复码一次性使用，迁移完成后旧设备上的云端备份会被删除、旧账本清零，避免同一份进度被复制给多个人。请像保管密码一样保管它。

三、广告

本版本不接入任何广告 SDK，游戏内不展示广告，也不存在“看广告领奖励”的入口。因此本游戏不收集广告标识符，不做广告归因，也不与广告平台共享任何信息。若未来版本引入广告，我们会在更新本政策并明确告知后才会启用。

四、应用内购买

iOS 版本提供可选的消耗型内购（铜钱补给、月卡）与外观类商品。支付由 Apple 通过 App Store 完成；交易完成后，我们的服务器会向 Apple 的交易接口核验这笔交易的真实性，再为对应玩家入账。我们不会接触也不会保存你的支付卡号、账单地址或 Apple 账户密码。

为了做到“同一笔交易不重复发放”、支持退款核对与换机恢复，服务器会保存：Apple 的交易标识、商品标识、入账时间、发放的铜钱或月卡天数，以及第二节所述的匿名设备标识。这些记录不用于广告或画像。你随时可以在 Apple ID 的购买记录中查看交易或申请退款。

五、可选的真人联机

联机对战是可选功能。当你主动进入联机时，客户端会向联机服务器发送房间号、座位、所选阵营、卡组构成、对局操作，以及断线重连所需的临时凭证；服务器在处理请求时会接收到你的 IP 地址等网络运行信息。这些信息仅用于完成对局匹配、同步与重连。不进入联机，就不会产生上述请求。

联机服务器会保留短期的运行日志用于排障，日志按滚动周期自动清理。对局数据不用于广告、画像或向你推送内容。

六、第三方服务与组件

iOS 版本通过系统提供的 StoreKit 完成内购，不集成第三方统计、广告或社交 SDK。云端备份与交易记录存放在 Turso（libSQL 数据库托管服务）位于境外的服务器上，仅由我们的服务端程序读写；我们与它之间是数据处理关系，它不会把这些数据用于自身目的。网页版不加载任何第三方统计脚本。若后续版本引入新的第三方组件，我们会在本政策中列明其名称与用途。

七、未成年人

本游戏不含用户生成内容与陌生人社交，联机仅限房间码邀请。建议监护人协助未成年人管理本机存档与系统商店的购买权限（可在 iOS「设置 → 屏幕使用时间 → 内容和隐私访问限制」中关闭 App 内购买）。

八、你的选择与权利

你可以随时在游戏内清除本机进度、关闭音效与音乐、不使用联机、不购买任何商品。删除本机数据后，本机进度无法恢复；如果云端还有备份，重装后会自动恢复。

如果你想删除云端备份与交易记录，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。由于本游戏没有账号，我们只能凭你提供的恢复码（若已生成）或设备内显示的匿名标识来定位数据；无法定位时我们无法执行删除。核验后我们会在 15 个工作日内删除，并回复确认。

九、历史账户数据

早期版本曾提供账号与云存档功能，该功能已下线，本游戏不再提供注册、登录或云同步。旧版本遗留在服务器上的账号身份、云存档、卡组与战报数据可能仍然存在，我们不会再把它们用于任何目的，也不会与当前游戏进度关联。

如你曾在旧版本注册，可经历史数据管理页验证原邮箱并申请删除本人的战报、卡组与游戏档案；该页面不注册新账号，也不会上传或下载当前游戏进度。

如你曾在旧版本注册并希望删除相关数据，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，并在邮件中注明原注册邮箱。我们核验后会处理。

需要说明的限制：自助删除覆盖旧版的战报、卡组与游戏档案三类数据，不包含登录用的认证身份本身；当年仅用短信或第三方账号登录的用户无法通过自助页验证。这类情况请直接发邮件，我们会单独处理。

十、政策更新与联系方式

本政策如有实质变更，我们会更新本页顶部的日期，并在游戏内提示。继续使用即表示你接受更新后的政策。

联系邮箱：ahshg686@163.com。我们通常在 7 个工作日内回复。

Privacy Policy — English

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · applies to v3.66 and later

Three Kingdoms Cards (Chinese title 三国卡兹) is a single-player card battle game. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no phone number, email or password required, and no advertising. This policy explains what we handle, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

1. What we do not collect

The game has no registration or sign-in and does not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera content. It does not read the clipboard, does not request the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across apps or websites. Audio plays locally and is never recorded or uploaded.

2. Where your progress is stored

On your device. Progress, decks, unlocked stages and stars, coins and cosmetics, settings and daily reward dates are stored in local storage under sanguo.profile.v1 and sanguo.decks.v1. The game is fully playable offline; progress made offline stays on the device.

Cloud backup, automatic when online. So that a reinstall or a new phone does not lose your save, the game uploads your most recent progress (stages and stars, owned generals, decks, skins) to our database provider as a backup. The backup is keyed to a randomly generated anonymous device identifier and contains no name, phone number, email, Apple account, card number or advertising identifier. It is not linked to your activity in other apps or websites. Each device keeps a single, latest backup; older ones are overwritten.

Moving to a new device. You can create a recovery code in the game and enter it on a new device to carry over progress, purchased coins and the pass. The code works once: after the transfer, the old device's cloud backup is deleted and its balance is zeroed, so the same progress cannot be copied to several people. Treat it like a password.

3. Advertising

This version bundles no ad SDK and shows no ads, and there is no “watch an ad for a reward” entry. We therefore collect no advertising identifier, perform no ad attribution, and share nothing with ad networks. If a future version introduces ads, we will only enable them after updating this policy and telling you in the game.

4. In-app purchases

The iOS version offers optional consumable purchases (coin bundles, monthly pass) and cosmetic items. Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store; once a transaction completes, our server verifies it against Apple's transaction API before crediting the player. We never receive or store your card number, billing address or Apple account password.

To prevent double-granting, to reconcile refunds and to support device migration, the server stores: Apple's transaction identifier, the product identifier, the credit time, the coins or pass days granted, and the anonymous device identifier described in section 2. These records are not used for advertising or profiling. Refunds are handled through your Apple ID purchase history.

5. Optional online play

Online duels are optional. When you choose to play online, the client sends a room code, seat, faction, deck composition, in-match actions and a temporary reconnection token to the match server; the server also receives network information such as your IP address while handling the request. This is used only to match, synchronise and reconnect your match. If you never open online play, no such request is made.

The match server keeps short-lived operational logs for troubleshooting, rotated and deleted automatically. Match data is not used for advertising, profiling or content targeting.

6. Third-party services and components

The iOS version uses Apple's StoreKit for purchases and does not embed third-party analytics, advertising or social SDKs. Cloud backups and transaction records are stored with Turso (a libSQL database hosting service) on servers outside your region, read and written only by our own server code; Turso acts as a data processor and does not use this data for its own purposes. The web build loads no third-party analytics scripts. If a later version adds a third-party component, we will list its name and purpose here.

7. Children

The game contains no user-generated content and no open social features; online play is limited to invite codes. Guardians should manage local saves and store purchase permissions — on iOS you can turn off in-app purchases under Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.

8. Your choices and rights

You can clear local progress at any time, mute sound and music, skip online play, and buy nothing. Once local data is deleted, local progress cannot be restored; if a cloud backup exists, it is restored automatically after reinstalling.

To delete your cloud backup and transaction records, contact us at the address below. Because there is no account, we can only locate your data through the recovery code you provide (if you created one) or the anonymous identifier shown in the app; if we cannot locate it, we cannot carry out the deletion. After verification we delete within 15 business days and confirm by reply.

9. Legacy account data

An early version offered accounts and cloud saves. That feature has been retired and the game no longer offers registration, sign-in or cloud sync. Account identities, cloud saves, decks and match records left on the server by older versions may still exist. We no longer use them for any purpose and do not link them to current game progress.

If you registered in an older version, you may verify your previous email on the legacy data page (web only) and request deletion of your own match records, decks and game profile. The page registers no account and neither uploads nor downloads current progress. App users can email us with the original registration address instead.

Limitation: self-service deletion covers legacy match records, decks and game profiles, but not the authentication identity used to sign in, and users who signed in only by SMS or a third-party account cannot verify themselves on that page. In those cases please email us and we will handle it individually.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and notify you in the game. Continued use means you accept the updated policy. Contact: ahshg686@163.com. We normally reply within seven business days.
