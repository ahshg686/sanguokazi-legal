Pack Odds Disclosure — English Summary

Cost. Single pull 10 coins, 10-pull 90 coins. Coins are earned only by playing (stage clears, match results). They cannot be purchased with real money, cannot be exchanged for cash or goods, and cannot be traded. The game is ad-supported (IAA) with no in-app purchases, no top-ups and no paywall.

Rates per card. God 1% · Elite 12% · Common 87%. Each card first rolls its rarity with these probabilities, then is drawn uniformly from that rarity pool (pool sizes: 7 / 15 / 23; 45 cards in total).

Pity. Each of the 10 cards in a 10-pull rolls independently. Only if all ten are Common, the last card is replaced by an Elite. The pity never produces a God card, so it does not inflate the 1% God rate. Single pulls have no pity. Pity does not carry across pulls.

Deterministic packs. The three packs in the Collection screen (and the daily free pack) are deterministic: their full contents are visible before purchase, with no randomness. They do not contain God cards — the pool covers 15 Elite and 23 Common cards (38 in total), while the 7 God cards are available only through the 1% God rarity roll in Section 2 above.
